----- re-check from the box width scans (S5-U09) -----
With the boxes centred, every scan put the box centre left of 0: -3.4, -4.0, -4.5, -4.5,
-4.0 deg (100 mm) and -2.9, -3.4, -2.9 deg (230 mm); mean about -3.7 deg. So at 500/2400 us
the sensor's 0 pointed about 4 deg right (it also explains the right end of the sweep
going a little too far). Both pulses moved +40 us (about 4 deg): B5_SERVO_MIN_US 540,
B5_SERVO_MAX_US 2440 (b5_config.h; the standalone tool uses the same values).
Trial at 1490 us (540/2440):
  - by eye, the sensor at 'c' (1490 us) pointed slightly LEFT; at 1450 us it looked straight;
  - 230 mm box scans: -27.3 to +33.0 deg (3 scans, identical), centre +2.9 deg, i.e. now
    about 3 deg RIGHT, and the edges moved unevenly (left +1.1, right +10.4 deg);
  - 100 mm box scans: the sensor read the box at 291-300 mm (box 25 cm from the car,
    probably measured from the car's front: + 45 mm to the sensor), and the readings
    alternated with the background (290-300 / 510-780 mm), so no clean edges.
Conclusion: the scans shift by +/-4 deg with box placement and the room, more than the
40 us correction, so they cannot set the centre to better than that. The eye check
(1450 us straight, twice) decides: reverted to 500/2400 us.
